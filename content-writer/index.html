Newsletter samples

Three connected editions on Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), written for an AI-literate audience: a trend explainer, a tactical how-to, and a metrics deep dive.

01Trend explainer
🔍 Google isn't sending you traffic anymore.

Quick one today. 👋

For 20 years, the goal was simple: rank #1 on Google, get the click. That's changing fast.

More searches now get answered directly by AI (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google's AI Overviews) before anyone reaches a website. The new goal isn't ranking. It's getting cited inside the answer itself. People are calling this AEO: Answer Engine Optimization.

Here's the uncomfortable part: you can rank #1 by old standards and still be invisible in an AI answer, because the model never quotes you. Some teams are already seeing click-through rates drop while brand mentions inside AI answers go up. Visibility and traffic are quietly splitting apart.

🔧 What's actually shifting:

  • Clear, direct answers near the top of a page beat slow build-ups
  • Structured data and FAQ formatting matter again
  • Being quotable is starting to matter as much as being linkable

👀 Worth watching: whether AI platforms start showing clear source credit. That's the difference between AEO becoming a real channel or just quietly absorbing everyone's content for free.

A couple of signals that things are already moving:

  • 🔹 Perplexity's publisher program expanded this month
  • 🔹 A few SEO tools have already rebranded parts of their stack around "AI visibility"

If your strategy is still 100% built around Google rankings, you might already be behind. More on what to actually do about it in the next edition.

See you in your inbox. 👋

02Tactical how-to
🚨 Your brand is invisible to AI. Here's the fix.

🔎 Try this: type your product category into ChatGPT right now. Is your brand in the answer? For most companies, no.

Ranking #1 on Google used to be enough. Now AI answer engines summarize and skip linking altogether. If your content isn't built to be pulled and cited, you don't exist in that answer, no matter how good your old SEO was.

✅ How brands are actually getting cited:

Answer fast. Put the actual answer in the first two sentences. Models pull from clear, self-contained answers, not slow intros. If a human has to scroll to get the point, so does the model.

Format like an FAQ, even when it isn't one. Headers phrased as questions ("What is X?") make it easy for AI to lift a direct answer and attach it to you.

Get mentioned elsewhere, not just linked. Reviews, comparison posts, Reddit threads all feed the "consensus" answer, even without a click.

Make claims verifiable. Vague marketing copy gets filtered out. Specific numbers and named features get pulled in.

As AI Overviews grow, "ranked well, zero clicks" is becoming the default outcome for content that isn't built this way. Visibility inside the answer is the new visibility.

A couple of signs this shift is already underway:

  • 🔹 Some brands are now tracking "share of AI voice" the way they used to track SEO rank
  • 🔹 Schema markup is quietly becoming table stakes again

If you haven't audited how your brand shows up in AI answers, that's this week's homework. Next up: how to actually measure this.

See you in your inbox. 👋

03Metrics deep dive
📊 You can't improve what you're not measuring.

📩 Last time: why your brand might be invisible in AI answers. Today: how do you actually know if it's working?

Traditional analytics weren't built for this. Google Analytics tracks clicks and sessions, but if someone sees your brand cited inside ChatGPT and never clicks through, your dashboard shows nothing. You could be winning at AEO and have zero proof.

📈 What teams are tracking instead:

Share of AI voice. Run your category's common questions through ChatGPT, Perplexity, and AI Overviews, then track how often, and how favorably, you show up versus competitors. Blunt, but useful.

Referral traffic from AI platforms. Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Copilot are starting to show up as traffic sources, even at low volume. A rising trend here is a real signal.

Citation tracking. Some SEO tools now flag when your content gets pulled into a generated answer, even without a click. Early days, but the direction's clear.

Branded search after AI exposure. If people start googling your brand name right after seeing it in an AI answer, that's a strong indirect signal, even if it never shows up cleanly in one metric.

Honestly, the tooling's still catching up. Anyone claiming a perfect AEO dashboard right now is overselling it. But tracking these four beats flying blind.

A couple of signs the tooling's starting to catch up too:

  • 🔹 A few SEO platforms quietly added "AI visibility" tabs recently
  • 🔹 Expect a wave of dedicated AEO analytics tools within the year

If you're still measuring success by organic clicks alone, you're missing half the picture.
